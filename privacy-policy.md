# Acme Components Privacy Policy

> **DEMO POLICY ONLY.** Acme Components is fictional in this sample.
> This page is provided to test the Standing dapp and is not legal
> advice or a statement of an actual organization's practices.

**Effective date:** 20 October 2026

## Data retention

Acme Components' demonstration policy states that personal data is
retained only for as long as needed for the stated purpose and, unless a
longer period is required by law, contract, or a documented dispute
hold, is deleted or anonymized within **90 days** after that purpose has
been completed.

## Data handled in this demonstration

The fictional policy may cover contact details, account records, support
communications, and basic service logs supplied during a demo.

## Access and deletion

In this sample policy, access to personal data is limited to authorized
personnel who need it for the stated purpose. Requests for access or
deletion would be handled through the organization's designated privacy
contact.

## Demo limitation

This document is a fictional example created solely to demonstrate a
policy statement containing a data-retention limit. It does not
establish that any real company follows these practices. A real
organization should publish its own reviewed and approved privacy
policy.
